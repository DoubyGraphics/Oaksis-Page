The name

Oak + oasis.

Oak gives us the first half: strength, patience, a root system you can’t see but can feel. Oasis gives us the second: relief, generosity, a place where new things have room to grow.

Put them together and you get our point of view: steadiness meets adaptability. The best identity is not a monument. It is a living structure with enough backbone to be recognised and enough air to respond.

“We make the kind of work that gets clearer with use.”

A grounded beginning

Small on purpose.

Oaksis began with a notebook, a laptop, and a simple frustration: too many brands were being made to perform instead of communicate.

We stayed small so the work could stay close. Today, we work with founders, cultural organisations, and thoughtful teams across Nigeria and remotely worldwide. No layers between the question and the person solving it.

Our context

Made in Nigeria.
Open to the world.

We are shaped by a place where a plan must be clear enough to survive a power cut, flexible enough for a changed road, and human enough to bring people along.

That means we value practical beauty. Systems that work on a phone. Language that sounds like a person. Ideas with a little weather on them.
